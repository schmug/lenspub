Riverside schools restore systems three weeks after ransomware attack

By a staff reporter of the fictional Riverside Ledger

The Riverside Unified School District said on Monday that its student information system is back online, three weeks after a ransomware attack forced teachers to take attendance on paper. District officials described the incident as the most serious school cybersecurity event in the county's history.

According to the district's incident report, the attackers entered through a vendor account that lacked multi-factor authentication. The report says no evidence has been found that student records were copied, though the investigation is continuing and families will be notified if that changes.

Correction: An earlier version of this story said the attack lasted two weeks. It lasted three weeks.

Parents at Tuesday's board meeting pressed for details on data privacy, asking which student data the vendor could access and how long it is retained. The superintendent promised a public data inventory by the end of the semester.

The weather service expects a mild weekend with light rain on Sunday afternoon, clearing by evening, and temperatures in the low sixties across the valley.

Tickets for the spring musical at Riverside High go on sale Friday at the box office, with matinee performances on both Saturdays of the run and a discounted price for seniors.
